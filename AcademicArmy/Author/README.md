# The Author

Author is the paper-writing agent. It drafts and iterates on the manuscript based on research goals, experimental results, Reviewer feedback, and outputs from the other agents.

Main responsibilities:

- Plan the paper structure, including the abstract, introduction, related work, method, experiments, discussion, and conclusion.
- Turn implementation details from Coding Team, result figures from Plotter, and explanatory illustrations from Illustrator into a coherent paper narrative.
- Revise the manuscript according to Reviewer feedback to improve logic, clarity, contribution framing, and academic writing quality.
- Keep terminology, notation, experiment descriptions, and contribution statements consistent across the paper.
